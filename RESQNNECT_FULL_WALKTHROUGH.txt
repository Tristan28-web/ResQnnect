2. THE COMMANDER'S MANUAL (ADMIN DEMO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Orchestrate a city-wide response and verify community safety.

✅ STEP 1: MONITOR THE STAT STREAMS
   - Look at the top cards. They pulse with live data.
   - See "Active Responders" (green) and "SOS Calls" (red) update in real-time.

✅ STEP 2: BROADCAST AN EMERGENCY
   - TAP the "Radar Alert" button.
   - Title: "Flood Warning - Zone 4"
   - Description: "All citizens near the river, please move to Safe Zones immediately."
   - Result: Every citizen and responder gets this alert instantly.

✅ STEP 3: LAUNCH THE CITY-WIDE CHECK-IN (High-Value Demo)
   - LONG-PRESS the "Radar Alert" button.
   - Protocol name: "Typhoon Protocol Alpha"
   - Result: This triggers a high-priority popup for EVERY citizen app city-wide.

✅ STEP 4: VISUALIZE THE HEATMAP
   - Navigate to "Safety Map" (Action Card).
   - Watch the map populate with Green Circles (Safe) and Red Icons (Needs Help).
   - Use this to tell your team where to prioritize search and rescue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE FIELD OPS GUIDE (RESPONDER DEMO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: High-speed mission execution and status tracking.

✅ STEP 1: GOING ON DUTY
   - Look at the "Tactical Dial". Tap to go "ON DUTY".
   - Your status turns glowing GREEN, and your location is now live for the Admin.

✅ STEP 2: THE ADVISORY FEED
   - Check the "System Advisories" section to see the Admin's latest broadcasts.
   - Stay informed without leaving your mission dashboard.

✅ STEP 3: MISSION NAVIGATION (Simulation)
   - When an Admin assigns a mission, the "Active Mission" card appears.
   - Tap "START NAVIGATION" to get the exact route to the distressed citizen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE CITIZEN SURVIVAL GUIDE (USER DEMO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Staying alive and contributing to community safety.

✅ STEP 1: THE SOS SIGNAL (The "Anti-Accident" Logic)
   - Tap and HOLD the SOS button for 3 seconds.
   - Watch the circular progress bar. 
   - Result: This prevents pocket-dialing while ensuring a rock-solid data 
     transmission to the Command Center with your exact location.

✅ STEP 2: REPORT A COMMUNITY HAZARD
   - Open the Map and tap the Orange "Hazard" Icon.
   - Report a "Deep Flood" or "Blocked Road" at your current position.
   - Result: You just helped your neighbors avoid a dangerous path.

✅ STEP 3: THE PROXIMITY ALERT (The "Automatic Shield")
   - While walking with the map open, if you approach a hazard reported by 
     someone else (~500m), an Orange Warning Banner will automatically 
     slide down: "HAZARD AHEAD: DEEP FLOOD". 

✅ STEP 4: THE RESCUE VAULT (Offline Survival Mode)
   - Tap "OFFLINE VAULT" on your dashboard.
   - EVEN WITHOUT INTERNET: Access First Aid guides and coordinates of 
     your nearest Evacuation Centers.
   - Background Sync: The app silently updates this "Vault" whenever 
     you have signal, so you're always ready for a blackout.
